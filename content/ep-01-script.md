# 1:20 — THE PROBLEM

**The informal chain today:**

Need a worker? → Friend → Relative → Contractor → WhatsApp → Phone call → Finally worker

Worker needs work? → Ask someone → Call someone → Go to a contractor → Maybe find work. Maybe not.

**Why can't these two sides simply find each other?**

---

# 2:10 — THE IDEA

YOGYA connects people who need work with people who are ready to work.

**Example:**
FARMER: "Need 5 workers, Hassan, Tomorrow, ₹600/day"
→ YOGYA shows available nearby workers
→ WORKER: "Available"
→ CONNECTION

---

# YOGYA 🤝

**The right person. The right work.**

> A local work marketplace connecting workers with employers across India — starting from Hassan, Karnataka.

---

## The Problem

Finding workers in India is still surprisingly informal:

```
Need a worker?
Friend → Relative → Contractor → WhatsApp → Phone call → Finally worker

Worker needs work?
Ask someone → Call someone → Maybe find work. Maybe not.
```

Two sides that need each other — with no efficient way to connect.

---

## The Idea

YOGYA is a platform where:

- A farmer posts: *"Need 5 workers, Hassan, tomorrow, ₹600/day"*
- Nearby available workers see it instantly
- Connection happens

**Categories:**
🌾 Agriculture · 🔧 Plumbing · ⚡ Electrical · 🧱 Construction · 🎓 Student part-time · 🎪 Events

**The twist:** You can be both. Today you hire. Tomorrow you work.

---

## What Makes This Different

Not just a job board. A local trust network where workers build verified profiles — ratings, completed jobs, experience — and customers find reliable help fast.

---

## Business Models (Under Exploration)

1. Transaction fee per hire
2. Workforce supply for businesses (margin)
3. Lead generation / subscriptions for providers
4. Agriculture seasonal workforce marketplace
5. Student part-time connector

---

## Current Phase

🔬 **Validation — before building anything**

**DON'T BUILD FIRST. VALIDATE FIRST.**

**Target (Hassan, first experiment):**
- 10 real workers
- 10 real customers
- 10 real jobs completed

**Questions to answer:**
- Do customers actually have this problem?
- How do they currently find workers?
- Would they trust a new platform?
- What would they pay?
- Would they come back?
